     238             :  * The initial multiplication also admits many seeds sharing its low

     242             :  *
     243             :  * The final XOR-fold has several useful degeneracies:
     244             :  * - A zero operand always produces zero.
     245             :  * - An operand of 2^64-1 produces an XOR-fold of 2^64-1 for any nonzero
     246             :  *   second operand.
